An opening paragraph describing what the engagement produces: not a content
calendar for its own sake, but a publishing rhythm sized to the team that has
to maintain it.

## What you get

- **The question map.** The questions buyers actually ask, ordered by how much
  they matter and how well you currently answer them.
- **The archive pass.** Which existing pages already answer those questions and
  what each one needs.
- **The rhythm.** How often to publish, who writes, and who signs off.

## How it works

1. **Listen.** Sales calls, support tickets, and forums — where the real
   questions live.
2. **Map.** Match questions to pages you already have.
3. **Plan.** A schedule the team can actually keep.

Teams with a real archive and not enough hands. Describe the fit here.
