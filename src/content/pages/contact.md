A note about what you are working on is plenty. This paragraph sets the
expectation for a reply: who reads it, and roughly how long it takes.

## What to include

- A link to your site
- The two or three competitors you measure yourself against
- What made you start looking for help now

None of it is required. A sentence works too.

## What happens next

Describe the first step here — a short reply, a call, or a short intake form —
