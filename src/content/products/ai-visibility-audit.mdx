## What you get

- **The answer log.** Buyer questions run through the major answer engines, with
  the raw responses and the sources each one cited.
- **The competitor picture.** Who gets recommended instead, and which of their
  pages earned it.
- **The ranked fix list.** Changes in priority order, each with an owner and an
  effort estimate.
- **The readout.** A call to walk through the findings, plus a written summary
  short enough to forward.

## How it works

1. **Kickoff.** Agree on the questions, the competitors, and who attends the readout.
2. **Fieldwork.** Run every question through every engine, more than once.
3. **Analysis.** Trace each citation back to the page that earned it.
4. **Readout.** Walk the team through the findings and the plan.
